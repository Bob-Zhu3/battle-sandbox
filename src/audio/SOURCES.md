# Field recordings

`field-sounds.mp3` contains short mono edits of the recordings below. The edits trim, filter, level, and sometimes retime individual performances. They are distributed under the original [CC0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Sound in the game | Recording |
| --- | --- |
| Bow under tension | Paveroux, [Bow Drawn](https://freesound.org/people/Paveroux/sounds/490556/) |
| Released string | saturdaysoundguy, [Arrow Loose](https://freesound.org/people/saturdaysoundguy/sounds/394185/) |
| Poison bottle and falling glass | IENBA, [Glass Bottle Breaking](https://freesound.org/people/IENBA/sounds/607910/) |
| Charge shouts | randbsoundbites, [short effort shouts](https://freesound.org/people/randbsoundbites/sounds/868443/) |
| Human hurt reactions | thebardofblasphemy, [voice takes](https://opengameart.org/content/grunts-male-death-and-pain), and AuraVoice / Nocturnal_Vanguard, [hurt reactions](https://opengameart.org/content/female-hurt-grunts-groans) |
| Creature calls, breaths and growls | rubberduck, [creature recordings](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx) |
| Sword and shield contact | Vehicle, [recorded metal clashes](https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-weapons-and-apparel-sfx-library) |
